## What is CFO Techstack?

CFO Techstack is a content and community platform helping finance leaders build, improve and scale the modern finance stack, with Xero at its core. Through practical content, independent research, real-world tech stack examples, events, our App Map and a community of finance leaders, we help teams understand their technology options, learn from their peers and make better decisions about the tools and workflows behind their finance function.

Our focus is on practical, community-led insight: what finance teams are actually using, how their stacks work in practice, and what others can learn from their experiences. Our insights and recommendations are community-sourced and never pay-to-play.

Founded by Mayday, CFO Techstack is an editorially independent initiative designed to support the wider finance community and help shape the conversation around the modern finance stack.

## About CFO Techstack

CFO Techstack was founded by Mayday in 2023 to plug a clear gap in the market: a community-led resource for finance leaders who want to build modern finance tech stacks with Xero at the core, without needing a costly ERP migration.

What started as a content and community hub has grown into a movement. Since then, we’ve published 100+ newsletter issues, hosted our first in-person Stacked conferences in London and Sydney, and grown to 13,000+ finance leaders actively building and sharing what modern finance teams (and modern tech stacks) look like in practice.

Run editorially independently, our insights and recommendations are always community-sourced and never pay-to-play.

Everything we do is guided by three principles.

1. Community First
‍
The community's interests come before commercial ones.
‍
It takes years to build trust, hours to lose it. Which is why at CFO Techstack we don't take shortcuts. This community is not pay-to-play, no vendor sits above the community, and there are no hidden agendas.
‍
Every decision we make must serve the community above all else.
‍
2. Raise the Bar
‍
Good enough isn't. As a community we hold ourselves to a high standard; in the content we produce, the events we run, the partners we work with, and the stories we tell. If we're putting the CFO Techstack name on something, it has to be worth the community's time.
And every time we do it again, it has to be better than the last.
‍
3. Give and Take
‍
This community thrives because people show up and share openly; real experiences, honest opinions, hard-earned knowledge. That peer-to-peer insight is unmatched, but requires everyone contributing.
‍
You get out what you put in.
‍
We take that seriously ourselves. We show up first with content, events, and connections worth your time. That's our commitment. In return, we ask that you give back what you can. And we’ll ensure you’re celebrated for doing so!

## What we do & key pages

- Homepage: https://www.getmayday.com/cfotechstack - The main CFO Techstack hub, bringing together the content, community, tools and resources available to finance leaders building modern finance stacks.
- App Map: https://www.getmayday.com/cfotechstack/app-market-map - A community-curated map of apps in the Xero ecosystem, organised by finance function to help finance leaders discover and compare tools for their stack.
- How I Stacked It: https://www.getmayday.com/cfotechstack/how-i-stacked-it - Real-world examples of finance leaders’ tech stacks, showing the tools they use, the problems they solve and the decisions behind their stack.
- Articles: https://www.getmayday.com/cfotechstack/article - Practical articles, insights and guidance covering finance technology, workflows, apps and the modern finance function.
- Consultant Directory: https://www.getmayday.com/cfotechstack/fts-consultant-directory - A directory connecting finance teams with consultants and specialists who can help them build, implement and optimise their Xero-based finance stack.
- Newsletter: https://www.getmayday.com/cfotechstack/newsletter - A free weekly newsletter covering finance technology, app insights, community stories and practical ideas for modern finance leaders.
- Stackademy: https://www.getmayday.com/cfotechstack/stackademy - CFO Techstack’s learning and certification platform, helping accountants, fractional CFOs and finance leaders understand how to build and implement scalable Xero-based tech stacks.
- StackChat: https://www.getmayday.com/cfotechstack/stackchat - CFO Techstack’s conversational content format; ask your difficult questions and get an expert reply.
- The Stack Exchange: https://www.getmayday.com/cfotechstack/the-stack-exchange - A Slack-based community where finance leaders can ask questions, share experiences, discuss apps and workflows, and learn directly from their peers.
- FLAC (Finance Leader Advisory Council): https://www.getmayday.com/cfotechstack/flac - CFO Techstack’s representative body for in-house finance teams, bringing finance leaders together to share their perspectives and help shape the future of the finance tech stack.
