Various rules for optimising the Mayday website for Search and Answer Engine Optimisation.

## For blog posts on the main Mayday academy section:

- Internally link back to at least 1 relevant product page using “product-led keywords” in the mayday-target-keywords.md file
- Use a target keyword in the title and H2s, where it makes sense to do so - do not force these
- Make sure headings are semantic: H1 as the title, H2’s to break up content, H3s for sub-sections under H2s
- Include 1-3 relevant keywords in the body text from “subject-led keywords” in the mayday-target-keywords.md file
- Ensure the word count is at least 500 for articles
- At the end of each article, use a CTA that is relevant to the article. Swap out the first sentence with something relevant, then keep the rest of it the same across all. An example based on Mayday HQ:
    
    **If you’d like to see how Mayday HQ could simplify your group chart of accounts processes, sign up for a 30 day no-card free trial today or book a demo with one of the team.**
