Mayday is most relevant for finance leaders, CFOs, Heads of Finance, Financial Controllers, and in-house finance teams at growing businesses that need more control, automation, and visibility around month end.

We have three core audiences:

1. In-house finance leaders
    1. Primary users of Mayday looking to modernise and automate the financial close
2. Fractional CFOs
    1. Trusted advisors who influence technology decisions across multiple clients
3. Practice partners
    1. Accounting firms that drive adoption through practice licenses and partner referrals

Mayday is useful for both small and medium market businesses.

The CFO Techstack community supports Mayday customers through the knowledge it brings in helping them scale without resorting to an ERP.

### Current Primary ICP:

Although we have customers across all three core audiences, generally most of our customers fit this ICP.

- In-house finance teams at growing businesses that need to close month end, produce management information and maintain control as complexity increases.

Typical characteristics:

- Uses Xero, QuickBooks Online or Intuit Enterprise Suite
- Has 2–10 people in their finance team, sometimes more
- Has brought finance in-house from an external accountant
- May be single-entity or multi-entity
- Feels the limits of spreadsheets but does not necessarily want an ERP

This doesn’t mean this is the only ICP we care about, but it does mean our messaging is naturally more appealing to this ICP.

## Targeting Criteria - In-house finance leaders

For the purposes of prospecting, we have a more detailed criteria to meet for in-house finance leaders.

| **Gates** | **How it's checked** |
| --- | --- |
| Runs Xero, QBO, or Intuit Enterprise Suite | Finance job advert naming the ledger; Xero/Intuit app store review; case study for a Xero/QBO-only app (Dext, ApprovalMax, Fathom, Joiin) |
| Has an in-house finance function | >2 directly employed finance title on LinkedIn |
| Not on a mid-market ERP | No NetSuite / Intacct / Dynamics 365 F&O / SAP in job ads, staff profiles, or implementation-partner case studies (*n.b. we may want to target ‘switching to Xero’ content in future, not for now).* |
| In our primary markets UK, Aus | Registered office / HQ against geographies |
| Above revenue floor | >£5m revenue to be safe. Inside the core revenue for a company to expect an in-house finance team.* 
Companies House filings (UK); funding and headcount proxies elsewhere |
| **Fits** | **Scores higher when** |
| Finance team size | 2-10 direct employees |
| Entity count | 2+ entities; group structure visible at Companies House |
| Business model | Subscription, retainer, or contracted revenue - anything generating deferred revenue and prepayments |
| Growth rate | Finance or total headcount up materially year on year |
| Recent institutional funding | Round closed in last 18 months |
| Ecosystem maturity | Already running 2+ Xero/QBO ecosystem apps - they buy tools |
| Job titles | Decision makers: Chief Financial Officer / CFO, Finance Director, Financial Controller, Head of Finance |
| **Trigger** | **Where it surfaces** |
| Finance role advertised naming Xero or QBO | Job boards, careers pages - single best signal, confirms ledger and timing at once |
| New FD, Head of Finance, or Financial Controller appointed | LinkedIn job changes; new leaders re-tool in their first 90 days |
| Funding round closed | Funding databases, press |
| New entity incorporated, or an acquisition | Companies House filings - multi-entity pain arrives on a known date |
| Financial year end approaching | Companies House accounting reference date |
| Finance lead departs | LinkedIn - the spreadsheet owner leaving creates urgency |
