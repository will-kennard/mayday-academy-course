Mayday should be described as a month-end automation platform for finance teams using Xero, QuickBooks Online and Intuit Enterprise Suite.

Avoid:

- calling Mayday a generic accounting platform
- implying Mayday replaces Xero or QuickBooks
- implying Mayday is only for multi-entity groups
- positioning ERP as the inevitable next step

For specific messaging around Mayday the following descriptions:

## Marketing Descriptions

Used for external facing descriptions of the business.

### Marketing Position

Mend Month End.

### Short Description

Mayday reduces the month-end process from days to hours for both single and multi-entity organisations.

### Medium Description

Mayday reduces the month-end process from days to hours for both single and multi-entity organisations. Built for teams using Xero, QuickBooks Online and Intuit Enterprise Suite, Mayday combines close coordination with automation for the workflows that slow finance teams down, from balance sheet recs and task tracking to intercompany, recharges, prepayments, deferred revenue and adjusting journals.

### Longer Description

Mayday reduces the month-end process from days to hours for both single and multi-entity organisations. Built for finance teams using Xero, QuickBooks Online and Intuit Enterprise Suite, Mayday gives teams one place to coordinate the close, track progress, manage tasks, review balance sheet recs, explain P&L variances and automate the workflows that usually live in time-consuming spreadsheets. From intercompany reconciliations and recharges to prepayments, deferred revenue, accruals and adjusting journals, Mayday helps teams save time, improve control and close with confidence.

## Practical Descriptions

For when a more practical description is required in a non-sales context.

### Position

The month-end automation layer for scaling finance teams.

### Short Description

Based on Xero, Quickbooks Online and Intuit Enterprise Suite, Mayday helps single and multi-entity organisations close month-end faster through a suite of AI-powered tools and automations.

### Medium Description

Mayday is for finance teams using Xero, QuickBooks Online or Intuit Enterprise Suite. It helps single and multi-entity organisations close faster by combining month-end coordination such as task management, balance sheet reconciliations, P&L variances and accruals, and close progress tracking, with automation for workflows such as intercompany reconciliations, recharges, prepayments, deferred revenue, and adjusting journals.

### Longer Description

Mayday is the complete month-end suite for finance teams using Xero, QuickBooks Online or Intuit Enterprise Suite. It helps single and multi-entity organisations close faster with a range of AI-powered tools and automations for month-end coordination such as task management, balance sheet reconciliations, P&L variances, accruals, and close progress tracking, with workflows such as intercompany reconciliations, recharges, prepayments, deferred revenue, and adjusting journals. Mayday helps thousands of organisations reduce month-end close from weeks to hours.
